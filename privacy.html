h•ddn real and h•ddn real (Hebrew) · Last updated October 28, 2026

This policy covers the Android apps h•ddn real and h•ddn real (Hebrew), created by Yair Sarig and presented by The Daily Magician.

The short version

The apps do not collect, store, send or share any personal information. There are no accounts, no ads, no analytics and no tracking.

What the apps do

Everything in the apps, including all text and photos, is built into the app and works offline. The apps do not request the internet permission or any other device permission.

Links you choose to open

When you tap an object name, the app asks your phone to open a Google Images search for that object in your web browser. That search happens in your browser, outside the app, and is covered by Google's own privacy policy. The app itself sends nothing.

Settings on your device

The apps may remember simple preferences, such as light or dark mode, on your device only. This information never leaves your device and is removed when you uninstall the app. It is excluded from cloud backups.

Children

The apps are made for magicians and are not directed at children. They collect no information from anyone.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new date.

Contact

Questions about this policy can be sent to the developer email address shown on the app's Google Play page.
